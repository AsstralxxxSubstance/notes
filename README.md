things i actually use. not a tutorial, just stuff that works for me.

### tools
- [Typefully] — Cleanest place to write and schedule posts/threads without distractions. Free plan is enough to start.
- [Grok (or ChatGPT)] — Turns my rough thoughts into shorter, clearer posts that still sound like me. Fast way to get better wording.

### prompts / workflows
- [Reply under bigger accounts in your niche
Verified users read replies. A useful, specific, or slightly spicy reply gets you in front of them.
Aim for 10–20 quality replies per day. This is the fastest way to get noticed by Premium accounts.]
- [Post useful or interesting things consistently
1–3 original posts per day. Short, clear, and something a Premium user would actually save or reply to.
Use Typefully + Grok so it stays easy.]
- [Make your profile convert
When a verified person clicks your name, they should instantly understand who you are and why to follow.
Clear bio + the pinned profile README / notes style helps.]
- [Get X Premium yourself (optional but helpful)
It gives your replies higher priority, so more verified people see them. Not required to grow, but it helps the reply game.]
